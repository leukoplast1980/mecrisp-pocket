Comes with an example: `lightshow.fs`, a light show that uses every part of the board.

- A Knight Rider scanner on pins 0-7 and a binary counter on pins 8-11
- A "breathing" pin 12 made with software PWM: watch it in the GPIO tab at 50 ms
- A blip on every step whose pitch follows the scanner, and a breathing screen
- Pin 15 as a button: tap P15 in the GPIO tab to switch modes (it vibrates and changes the waveform and speed)

It opens in the editor on the first start; after that it is under Files ("Example: lightshow.fs"). Send it with Send > Whole file, type `lightshow`, and press Send with an empty line to stop it.
