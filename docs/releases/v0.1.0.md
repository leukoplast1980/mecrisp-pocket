First preview of Mecrisp Pocket: the real Mecrisp-Quintus Forth running on an emulated RISC-V board on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer).

- **REPL** with word completion, history, extra keys and a raw terminal mode
- **Editor** with Forth syntax colouring, completion, undo/redo, find, and sending a line, a selection or a whole file to the board (it stops at the first error and marks the line)
- **Registers** view with Pause and Step
- Words compiled with `compiletoflash` survive restarts; open files and the console come back after a restart

Not in this release yet: vibration, brightness and tones, the GPIO view, `include`, and the USB GPIO breakout.
